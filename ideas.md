# Design brief — Clearline Editorial Fintech

## Theme Name
Clearline Editorial Fintech

## Intro
A confident, editorial financial planning interface that makes borrowing decisions feel calm, legible and human. The product moves from a dark glass dashboard to a bright paper-like canvas with clean navy typography, white cards and a restrained teal/cobalt system.

## Design movement
Light editorial fintech: generous white space, thin rules, quiet shadows, compact data labels and a premium digital-banking tone. It should feel closer to a well-designed financial journal than a neon trading terminal.

## Core principles
1. Put the decision first: the result and its assumptions are easy to scan.
2. Use color sparingly: teal signals trust and positive progress, cobalt identifies interaction, violet identifies optional AI context, and amber/coral communicate attention.
3. Prefer clarity over decoration: white surfaces, readable type, and short supporting copy.
4. Make privacy and accessibility visible in the flow, not buried in a footer.

## Color philosophy
The base is warm cloud (#f6f8fb) with paper-white cards (#ffffff), deep navy ink (#12233d), slate body copy (#5b6e84), and mist rules (#dce4ed). Teal (#008f83) is the signature trust color. Cobalt (#2f6fed) supports controls, violet (#7358d4) marks AI, amber (#ad6e08) highlights caution, and coral (#d33e57) handles blockers.

## Layout paradigm
A responsive single-page editorial flow with a centered 1180px measure, asymmetric hero composition, thin section rules, and card clusters that feel tactile but not glossy. Forms remain dense enough for efficient entry, while result panels use generous white space and strong numeric hierarchy.

## Signature elements
- A shield-and-check monogram in teal/navy.
- A fine ruled-grid background that fades into the page.
- Circular score gauges with white centers and crisp colored arcs.
- Small uppercase eyebrow labels and tabular numeric outputs.
- Teal hairlines and cobalt focus states instead of glow-heavy borders.

## Interaction philosophy
Immediate feedback on fields and calculators, but never surprise the user with irreversible actions. AI requests use explicit loading states and always have a deterministic fallback. Keyboard focus rings, reduced-motion mode, and live status regions remain first-class behaviors.

## Animation
Short slide-up reveals, gentle opacity transitions, counter/gauge interpolation, and a subtle lift on cards. The motion is deliberately quieter than the prior neon direction and is disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Typography system
Inter from Google Fonts, falling back to system-ui. Heavy navy headings create hierarchy; regular slate body copy keeps the tone approachable. Numeric outputs use tabular numbers for stable alignment.

## Brand essence
Clear, composed, useful.

## Brand voice
Practical, plain-spoken, non-judgmental, and transparent about uncertainty. The app explains signals without promising outcomes and repeats that the experience is educational rather than professional advice.

## Wordmark/logo
The wordmark remains “loancheck” with the shield-and-check mark. The same mark is used in the header, favicon, and project icon.

## Signature brand color
Teal (#008f83), used for trust signals, active states, and positive outcomes.

## Asset decision
No hero photography is needed. The identity is carried by the flat logo, inline SVG icons, clean editorial surfaces, and data visualizations.
